About Abhinaya

Maestro in Carnatic Music

Welcome to Sri Vinaya School of Music, where traditional Carnatic music meets contemporary teaching methods. As the founder and principal instructor, I, Abhinaya Ranganathan, bring over a decade of musical expertise to nurture the next generation of musicians.

Located in the cultural heart of Puzhudivakkam, Chennai, our school offers comprehensive training in Carnatic vocal techniques and Bhajan performances for students of all ages and backgrounds.

My Musical Journey

  • Distinction holder in Tamil Nadu government technical music examination
  • Performed at various prestigious music academies and temples
  • Proficient in light music with wins in reality shows including Sapthaswarangal, Raagam Sangeetham
  • Experienced in live singing for Bharathanatyam recitals
  • Multiple inter-school and college competition winner
  • Core member of SRM College Music Band
  • Voice rendered for an album song (post-production ongoing)
